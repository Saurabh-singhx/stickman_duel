Stickman Duel - Online 2D Sword Fighting Browser Game

Play free cinematic stickman sword duels online with friends or challenge responsive AI bots in your browser.

Game Modes

  • Quick Play: Jump instantly into arena combat with customizable stages and difficulty levels.
  • Online 1v1 Room Codes: Host or join real-time multiplayer sword matches with friends using peer-to-peer WebRTC lockstep netcode.
  • Local 2-Player: Battle head-to-head on the same keyboard or with dual gamepad controllers.
  • Boss Rush: Test your parry mastery against a gauntlet of lethal sword-wielding bosses.
  • Endless Waves: Defeat escalating waves of stickman duelists and set high scores.
  • Training Dojo: Hone your combos with live frame data, hitboxes, parry windows, and dummy playback.
  • Daily Boss Fights: Daily seeded arenas and unique boss duels with persistent best scores.

Combat Controls

A/D to move, Space to jump, C to crouch, Shift to dodge, Left Click/J for light slashes, Right Click/K for heavy slashes, C + Right Click for unblockables, L to parry and block, U to grab, and E for specials.
